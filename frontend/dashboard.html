Ground Station Operational Guide

1. USB Serial Connection: Click CONNECT to stream live telemetry from USB UART (ESP32/STM32/LoRa) at 115200 baud via Web Serial API.

2. Flight Replay & Tests: Click REPLAY SUITE in the sidebar to open the 10 flight profile test suite or upload your own CSV file.

3. Interactive 3D Model: Click and drag the CanSat in its tile to orbit and inspect orientation. Mouse wheel zooms.

4. Tile Maximization: Click the expand icon in the header of any card to zoom that visualization to full screen.

5. CSV Data Export: Click DOWNLOAD DATA anytime to download an RFC 4180 compliant CSV recording.
